Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect the minimum needed to run this site and send our newsletter, we never sell your data, and third-party affiliate links may set their own cookies.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Pixel & Parsec ("we," "us," or "this site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit pixel-parsec.vercel.app, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

We keep data collection deliberately minimal. Here's everything that could be gathered while you browse:

  • Newsletter email address: If you voluntarily submit your email through our newsletter signup form, we store it solely to send you our weekly roundup. We do not require an account or any other personal details to subscribe.
  • Standard analytics data: Like most websites, we may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, approximate location (city/country level), device and browser type, and referring site. This data is anonymized or pseudonymized where possible and is used only to improve the site.
  • Cookies: We use a small number of first-party cookies for basic site functionality (like remembering your theme preference) and may use analytics cookies to measure traffic patterns.

How we use your information

Any information collected is used exclusively to operate and improve Pixel & Parsec: sending newsletter emails to subscribers who opted in, understanding which articles and gear guides are useful to readers, and diagnosing technical issues. We do not use your data for anything beyond these purposes.

Third-party affiliate links & cookies

Pixel & Parsec is an affiliate content site. Many of our "Check Price" buttons and product links lead to third-party retailers through affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program and other gaming/tech and pop-culture affiliate networks. When you click these links:

  • The retailer (e.g., Amazon) may set its own tracking cookie to attribute a purchase to Pixel & Parsec, which is how we earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • We do not control these third-party cookies, and their use is governed by the privacy policy of the retailer or network in question, not by us.
  • We may receive limited, aggregated click and conversion data from affiliate networks — never your name, payment details, or shipping address.

For more on how these partnerships work editorially, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Full stop. Any data shared with service providers (like our email delivery platform or analytics tool) is shared solely so they can perform the service we've asked of them, under their own confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Data retention & your choices

Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email we send, which removes your address from our mailing list. If you'd like your data deleted sooner, or have questions about what we hold, just reach out using the contact details below.

Children's privacy

Pixel & Parsec is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally as the site evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us
