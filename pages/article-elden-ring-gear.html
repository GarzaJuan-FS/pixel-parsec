The Elden Ring & Dark Souls Gear Guide

Home / Gaming & Tech / Elden Ring & Dark Souls Gear Guide
By the Pixel & Parsec team · 9 min read · Updated 2026

Nobody needs special gear to beat a Souls game. People have done it with dance mats and bananas. But after several hundred hours split between Elden Ring, the Dark Souls trilogy, and Sekiro, I've found a handful of upgrades that genuinely reduce the number of deaths caused by input lag, hand fatigue, or just not hearing a tell over game audio — as opposed to deaths caused by, you know, being bad at the game. That second category is on you. This guide only covers the first.

Why gear actually matters here

FromSoftware's combat is built on frame-perfect windows — parries, rolls, weapon arts that only work if your input registers when you think it does. A worn-out analog stick with drift, or a controller with noticeable input lag, will cost you fights you should have won. This isn't about getting a competitive edge; it's about removing self-inflicted handicaps.

A quick word on PC builds

If you're playing on PC, these titles are mercifully not hardware-hungry by 2026 standards — a mid-range card from the last three years will hold 1440p/60 comfortably. Where the money is better spent is the peripherals below, not another GPU tier you won't notice in this specific genre.
