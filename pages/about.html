About

Built by a fan. Run like one, too.

Pixel & Parsec started as a group chat habit — sending friends links to whatever collectible, anime, or piece of gear I'd just gotten obsessed with that week — before it turned into an actual site. I'm not a media company. There's no editorial board, no content calendar built by a marketing team, no ghostwriters. It's one person who has genuinely spent a stupid amount of money on Star Wars shelf displays, has strong and probably annoying opinions about which Souls controller doesn't drift, and has watched way more anime than anyone should while claiming it's "research."

The name is exactly what it sounds like: a pixel for the games and screens, a parsec for the space-opera side of the brain that will not stop thinking about hyperspace lanes and orbital mechanics even though I have a day job. Three pillars, one obsession level.

What you'll actually get here

Every product mentioned on this site is something I've either owned, used at length, or researched deeply enough that I'd stake my own credibility on the recommendation. I skip products that don't hold up, even if they'd be an easy affiliate link. If a $300 recommendation isn't meaningfully better than a $50 one for what you're doing, I'll say so — that's the whole point of a site written by a fan instead of a brand.

How this site makes money

Pixel & Parsec is supported by affiliate commissions — mainly through the Amazon Associates program and a handful of gaming/tech affiliate partnerships. When you click a "Check Price" button and buy something, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It doesn't change what I recommend; it just keeps the servers running and the collectible budget alive. Full details are on the affiliate disclosure page.

Say hi

If you've got a recommendation, a correction, or just want to argue about the best Souls build, the contact link is in the footer. I read everything, even the arguments about lightsaber hilt ergonomics.
